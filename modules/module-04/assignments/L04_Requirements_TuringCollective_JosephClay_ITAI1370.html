📋 Requirements — All 7 Steps
1

Research and Identification: Conduct research to identify 6–8 major milestones in the evolution of rendering technology. This might include the invention of ray tracing, the introduction of programmable shaders, the development of Pixar's RenderMan, the advent of real-time ray tracing, among others.

2

AI-Generated Visuals: Utilize a generative AI system like DALL-E to create visuals for each milestone. Craft detailed prompts that describe the technology and its innovation at the time. For instance, for ray tracing, you might describe a scene illustrating the calculation of light paths through pixels in an image plane, demonstrating the realism it brings to computer graphics.

3

Curation and Timeline Assembly: From the generated images, select the most representative visual for each milestone. Arrange these images in chronological order to form a coherent visual timeline. This timeline should clearly depict the progression and evolution of rendering technologies.

4

Descriptive Annotations: Write 1–2 sentences for each milestone image, explaining its significance in the history of computer graphics and rendering. Highlight how each technological advancement contributed to the field and why it was considered a game-changer at its time.

5

Reflective Analysis: Evaluate the effectiveness of the AI-generated images in encapsulating the essence of each milestone. Discuss whether the visuals aided in understanding the historical progression of rendering technology. Reflect on the strengths and limitations of using a generative AI for this educational purpose.

6

Report Preparation: Combine the timeline, images, descriptions, and reflective analysis into a cohesive report. This report should not only be informative but also engaging, effectively communicating the evolution of a complex technical field through the creative use of AI-generated imagery.

7

Submission: Ensure your final report is well-organized, clearly written, and visually appealing.

💡 Considerations from Prof. Devarakonda
⚠️ Keep These in Mind
  • Focus on selecting milestones that represent significant leaps or turning points in rendering technology
  • Be creative with your AI prompts to generate imaginative yet informative visuals
  • Ensure that the visuals and descriptions are accessible and understandable to an audience that might not have a technical background in computer graphics
