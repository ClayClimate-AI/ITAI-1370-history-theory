Objective
The objective of this assignment is to familiarize you with the fundamentals of neural networks and their behavior. You will use TensorFlow Playground, an interactive web-based tool, to experiment with various neural network configurations and gain insights into how different parameters affect the performance of these networks.
Introduction to TensorFlow Playground
Research/Review the material on neural networks, their basic components, and how they work. (You can refer to the class slides to start then add other references, including tools such as GPT or Google Bard, just make sure that info can be verified.)

Access the TensorFlow Playground website: https://playground.tensorflow.org
Exploration Phase — 5 Tasks
1
Activation Functions
  • Create a neural network with one hidden layer and experiment with different activation functions (e.g., ReLU, sigmoid).
  • Discuss and record the observed effects on the network's performance.
2
Hidden Layer Neurons
  • Change the number of neurons in the hidden layer and observe how it affects the network's performance.
  • Discuss and record your observations.
3
Learning Rate
  • Adjust the learning rate and explain its impact on convergence speed and accuracy.
  • Document your findings and any insights.
4
Data Noise
  • Introduce noise in the data by changing the "Noise" slider and discuss how it affects the network's ability to generalize.
  • Present your observations and analyze the impact.
5
Dataset Exploration
  • Explore different datasets available in TensorFlow Playground and analyze how the network performs on each.
  • Document your findings for each dataset.
Report
Collaborate to create a report summarizing your group's findings. Include the following:
  • A brief introduction to neural networks and their components.
  • A description of each task, along with screenshots or visualizations.
  • Detailed observations and explanations for the effects of parameter changes.
  • A discussion of the practical implications of your findings.
  • Remember to cite your sources and add a reference list.
  • Base your report format on the Technical report sample on Module 00.
Conclusion
Conclude your assignment by reflecting on what your group has learned about neural networks and their behavior through this hands-on experience.
