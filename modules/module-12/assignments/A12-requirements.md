**Objective:** Critically analyze the concept of prediction in AI agents
and creatively envision a future where AI prediction shapes narrative
experiences.

## Assignment Description

1. **Theory Exploration**
   - Study how AI agents use historical data and patterns to make
     predictions (predictive modeling).
   - Understand AI's role in forecasting outcomes (finance, weather,
     health).

2. **Film/TV Series Analysis** — watch a film/series where predictive AI
   is central. Suggestions:
   - *Person of Interest* (TV)
   - *Minority Report* — pre-crime prediction, moral/legal implications
   - *I, Robot* (2004) — AI predicting human behavior, preemptive action
   - *Eagle Eye* (2008) — AI using data to predict and manipulate human
     action

   Identify how AI prediction influences the storyline and character
   decisions.

3. **Envisioning Predictive Narratives**
   - Create a storyline for a short film/episode centered on an AI agent
     with advanced predictive capabilities.
   - Weave in **at least 3** AI predictive-modeling concepts (e.g., risk
     assessment, trend analysis, behavior prediction).

4. **Impact Assessment**
   - Discuss potential impacts of predictive AI agents on human
     decision-making and privacy.
   - Reflect on the balance between the benefits of predictive insight and
     the risks of determinism.

## Deliverables
- Written report (3–4 pages) on predictive modeling in AI and its
  depiction in the chosen media.
- Original storyline (2–3 pages) showcasing an AI predictive narrative.
- Reflection paper (1–2 pages) on societal/ethical considerations of
  predictive AI agents.

**Assessment Criteria:** comprehension of AI prediction/modeling
techniques · originality/integration of AI concepts into the storyline ·
depth of insight into ethical/societal ramifications · research and
references.
