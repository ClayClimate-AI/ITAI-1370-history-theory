Part 1 — Exploration of Computational Humor Models
1

Research and analyze the following computational humor models, highlighting their evolution from rule-based frameworks to contemporary AI-driven systems:

HaHaAcronym
Examine how this model uses linguistic templates and semantic relations to craft humorous acronyms.
STANDUP
Explore this interactive system's role in aiding children with communication impairments through the generation of punning riddles.
Jape
Study this early model's approach to creating punning riddles and its reliance on rule-based methods.
BERT-Based Humor Detection
Investigate the application of advanced NLP models like BERT in identifying humor within text.
The HumorHawk System
Analyze the implementation of deep learning techniques in generating and assessing humorous content in this modern system.

a. Purpose: Clearly define the system's main function (e.g., joke creation, humor identification).

c. Strengths & Limitations: Critically assess the areas where the system excels and its potential shortcomings.

Ensure to support your analysis with appropriate references to primary sources, highlighting the significance of these models in the field.
