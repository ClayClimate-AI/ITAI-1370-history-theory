**Due:** Today (Aug 1), 11:59pm · **Points:** 100

**Objective:** Understand AI digital assistants and conversational AI apps,
analyze their capabilities and ethical implications, and explore how the
movie *Her* (2013) reflects our relationship with these technologies.

## Instructions

1. **Research & Reading**
   a. Overview of what AI digital assistants/conversational AI apps (like
      ChatGPT) are and their current capabilities.
   b. How they operate — the technology propelling them, the data they use.
   c. Articles/documentaries on the ethical implications of AI digital
      assistants and conversational AI platforms.

2. **Watch "Her" (2013)** — dir. Spike Jonze. Focus on the relationship
   between Theodore and the AI, Samantha.

3. **Analysis**
   a. **Description of AI in "Her"** — enumerate Samantha's features and
      compare with current assistants (Siri, Alexa, Google Assistant) and
      conversational platforms (ChatGPT).
   b. **Emotional Connection** — how the film depicts the emotional bond
      between humans and AI; debate whether this mirrors current
      interaction with AI assistants.
   c. **Ethical Implications** — reflect on the ethical considerations the
      film raises about cultivating advanced AI, paired with research.
   d. **Future of AI** — given the film and current trends, forecast the
      trajectory of AI digital assistants/conversational AI.

4. **Presentation**
   a. Format: PowerPoint, Prezi, or a comprehensive essay.
   b. Introduction: overview of AI digital assistants/conversational AI +
      brief overview of *Her*.
   c. Body: engage with the Step 3 analysis; multimedia encouraged (movie
      excerpts, diagrams, sound bytes).
   d. Conclusion: recap evaluation/prognostications, pros/cons of AI
      digital assistants and conversational AI in daily life.

5. **Discussion Questions** — 3–5 open-ended questions tying back to the
   themes discussed, to foster classroom dialogue.

6. **Citations** — all references, including the movie, properly cited
   (APA or MLA).

7. **Submission** — forward by due date; be ready to discuss analysis and
   questions in class.
