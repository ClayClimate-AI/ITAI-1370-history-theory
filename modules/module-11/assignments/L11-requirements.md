# L11 — Comparing AI Assistants Experiment

**Due:** Today (Aug 1), 11:59pm · **Points:** 100

The field of conversational AI is rapidly evolving. Get hands-on experience
interacting with different AI assistants and compare their capabilities.

## Instructions

1. **Select at least 2** of: Alexa, Siri (Apple), Google Assistant, Cortana
   (Microsoft), Bixby (Samsung), Portal from Facebook.

2. **Develop 3–5 open-ended questions or commands** to test each assistant
   — chosen to explore conversational ability, not just fact lookup.
   Examples: "What is your favorite movie?", "Tell me a joke.", "Have a
   conversation with me."

3. **Record observations** for each assistant on:
   - How natural/conversational it seems
   - Ability to understand context and hold coherent dialogue
   - Tone, personality, sense of humor (or lack thereof)
   - Any frustrating or confusing interactions

4. **Write a 3–4 page paper** comparing the experience across assistants.
   Address:
   - Which assistant felt the most human-like? Why?
   - Which was the most limited? What gave it trouble?
   - How have the assistants improved compared to expectations?
   - How might they continue to advance in the future?

5. Cite sources and provide examples of actual interactions with each
   assistant.

Focus the analysis on how "human-like" the assistants are and how their
specific strengths/limitations reflect the underlying technology.
