**Objective:** Engage with AI/robotics by conceptualizing a robot design
for a specific application, and critically evaluate the ethical
implications of that design in real-world scenarios.

## Instructions

1. **Choose an Application Domain** — an area where robots are already
   used or have future potential (e.g., healthcare, manufacturing,
   domestic assistance, exploration, entertainment).

2. **Design Your Robot**
   - Description: detailed — intended functions, physical appearance,
     unique features.
   - Sketch: hand-drawn or digitally created (generative AI allowed) —
     doesn't need to be artistically advanced, just clearly convey the
     design.

3. **Functionality and AI Integration**
   - How is AI integrated? What tasks does it perform autonomously? How
     does it interact with humans/its environment?
   - What sensors/input methods (cameras, microphones) does it use to
     perceive its surroundings?

4. **Ethical Analysis**
   - Identify and discuss the ethical implications (privacy, safety, job
     displacement, societal impact).
   - Propose solutions/guidelines to mitigate negative ethical
     implications.

5. **Presentation** — compile findings into a well-organized report or
   presentation; include the sketch and a clear explanation of
   functionality and ethical considerations.

## Research Component (required)
- **AI and Robotics Fundamentals** — e.g. Robin Murphy, *Introduction to
  AI Robotics*.
- **Ethical Considerations in AI** — ethical principles/guidelines
  (transparency, privacy, accountability, fairness); suggested: "The
  global landscape of AI ethics guidelines" (Nature Machine Intelligence),
  "Ethics of Artificial Intelligence" (UNESCO).
- **Recent Developments** — recent academic publications, e.g. ArXiv's
  "Ethics of AI: A Systematic Literature Review of Principles and
  Challenges."

Use the research to inform the robot's design/functionality, apply
ethical principles to the analysis, and cite all sources used.
