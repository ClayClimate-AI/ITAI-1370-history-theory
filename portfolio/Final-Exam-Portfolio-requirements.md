# Final Exam Portfolio

**Due:** Aug 8, 11:59pm · **Points:** 100 · **Individual assignment · 1 attempt allowed**

The Final Exam is an **Individual** Portfolio for the course. For each
Module, record activities and results (what was learned from the
experience). Include any tables, graphs, and images as appropriate.
Screenshot images are acceptable. Links to any valuable articles/videos
may also be included. Include citations to sources of intellectual
property used. To conserve storage space, convert the completed file to a
PDF for submission.

**Filename:** `FE_YourName ITAI 1371 2023` — ⚠️ this filename convention
on Canvas literally says "ITAI 1371 2023," which looks like a leftover
from a prior course/semester. Verify the correct filename with the
professor before final submission, per MASTER-TRACKER.md's existing flag
on this.
